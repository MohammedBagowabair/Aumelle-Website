About Aumelle

Slow, golden, yours.

You know the feeling. Warm towels, low light, the hush of a treatment room, and an hour where nobody needs anything from you. Then you drive home, and it's gone by dinner.

Aumelle teaches you to create that feeling yourself, at home, with what you already have. The name is inspired by the French au miel, "with honey": warm, slow and golden, the way self-care should feel.

The signature rituals

Six rituals. One golden habit.

Each week you add one signature ritual, from the first mist of the morning to the last candle of the night. By the end, you'll have a complete home spa routine that's entirely yours.

  1. i.Week 1

    Morning Mist

    Cool water, a fine facial mist and a slow minute at the window to wake skin and senses.

  2. ii.Week 2

    The Glow Hour

    An at-home facial: cleanse, mask and a gentle gua sha sequence for that after-spa glow.

  3. iii.Week 3

    Petal Soak

    A bathhouse-style soak with salts, petals and warm towels. No bathtub? There's a shower version.

  4. iv.Week 4

    The Slow Layer

    Turn your skincare into a massage: serum and cream pressed in slowly, the way a therapist would.

  5. v.Week 5

    Tea Pause

    A midday pause with a warm cup, phone away, that resets a busy day in a few quiet minutes.

  6. vi.Week 6

    Evening Hush

    A candlelit wind-down that turns the hour before bed into a ritual, not a scroll.

Your free ritual · The Warm Palm Press

Three minutes of warmth.

A taste of Aumelle you can try tonight, and the ritual we'll send you the moment you join.

  1. Warm. Rub three drops of facial oil, or your usual cream, between your palms until they feel warm.
  2. Press. Cup your palms over your face and hold for three slow breaths. Feel the warmth sink in.
  3. Glide. Sweep your hands from the centre of your face out to your ears, then down the sides of your neck.
  4. Repeat three times, then sit still for a moment before you open your eyes.

Questions

Good to know.

Anything else on your mind? Join the waitlist and simply reply to your welcome email. A real person reads every message.

When does Aumelle launch?
Very soon. The launch date goes to the waitlist first, before it's shared anywhere else.
How much will it cost?
Pricing will be announced at launch. Waitlist members hear it first and get first access, so joining now is the best way to make sure you don't miss out.
Do I need special products or tools?
No. Every ritual works with what you already have: a basin or shower, a towel, your usual skincare and a quiet corner. Optional extras, like a gua sha stone or bath salts, are suggested, never required.
How much time does it take?
Most rituals take 20 minutes or less. Short on time? Each one has a five-minute version for busy days.
I'm a complete beginner. Is it for me?
Absolutely. Nothing is extreme or complicated. Each ritual is explained step by step, and you can go at your own pace.
What will you do with my email?
We'll only use it to send you your welcome ritual and news about Aumelle. We never sell or share it, and you can unsubscribe with one click at any time.
